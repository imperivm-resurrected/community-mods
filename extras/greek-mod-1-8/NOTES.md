# Greek Civilization Mod 1.8 (Rattlesmake)

Row 4. `GreekMod_1.8.rar` (2.7 GB, forum t1207) is a full copy of the game (1.40 base): `Packs\*.pak` (17, with `zGreek.pak` over `data.pak`), `Local\{czech,english,italian,spanish}.pak`, its own `rle.mmp` (438 MB), `Movies`, `Music`, `Scenarios`, `Adventures`, `Conquests`, plus `gbr.exe`, the launcher and DLLs.

**This port is Greece as a civilization of its own** (AGENTS.md, *Civilizations are independent*), with the maps, adventures, videos, music and menu skin of the mod. The game's 8 civilizations are untouched. What the mod did to them is listed under *Left out*.

## Rebuild

```bash
mkdir originals/greek-mod-1.8-full && bsdtar -xf originals/forum/t1207/files/GreekMod_1.8.rar -C originals/greek-mod-1.8-full
node tools/flatten-paks.js originals/greek-mod-1.8-full/GreekMod_1.8 originals/greek-mod-1.8-flat
ln -s $PWD/originals/greek-mod-1.8-full/GreekMod_1.8/rle.mmp originals/greek-mod-1.8-flat/rle.mmp
node tools/port-140.js originals/greek-mod-1.8-flat ported/greek-mod-1-8 --id greek-mod-1-8 --name "Greek Civilization Mod 1.8" --version 1.8 --category Civilizations
node tools/greek-1-8.js
node tools/greek-1-8-civs.js
```

`shrink-mod.js` is not used: it compares against the HD game and would undo its fixes (this mod is 1.40). `tools/flatten-paks.js` unpacks the packages in name order (the greater name wins, as in the game) and lays the mod's loose files over them. `port-140.js` merges with HD. `tools/greek-1-8.js` then makes Greece independent (below). `ported/greek-mod-1-8/` is not in git. It needs `originals/game-data/paks/` with every HD pack extracted (README, *Setup*), `Minimap.pak` and `Fonts.pak` included.

## What `tools/greek-1-8.js` does

- **Own names.** Greece in 1.8 sat in Imperial Rome's slot with the letter `H`. Here it is `greek18` (`civs/greek18.json`, index 32, letter `Q`, `internalName` `Greek18`, template `Greek Rattlesmake`, `ai: true`). 129 names change one letter for another: classes (`HHoplite` → `QHoplite`, `HeroHHoplite` → `HeroQHoplite`, `GreekHero` → `QGreekHero`), commands (`trainHHoplite` → `trainQHoplite`, `hireheroH` → `hireheroQ`, `trainpeasanth` → `trainpeasantq`), entity folders and sprites (`Units/HHOPLITE` → `Units/QHOPLITE`), icons, voices and sound entities. The engine builds names from the letter (`GetRaceStrPref`), so a literal `"H"` in a script is changed too. The classes have `race="greek18"`.
- **Base classes.** The 1.8 edited the game's `Melee`, `Ranged`, `Hero`, `Horse`, `Unit`, `BaseVillage`…, which every civilization inherits. Those edits are written **inside each Greek class** (`<!-- changes the 1.8 made to its base classes -->`, 149 elements); the game's base classes stay as they are.
- **The Greek walls, gates and tower** the mod drew over Imperial Rome's `M*` ids are `Q*` classes (`QWallsN`, `QGate0`, `QTower`); Imperial Rome keeps the game's. `QTavern`, `QTavern2` and `QArena` (the city builder asks for them by letter) are copies of the Agora and the Gymnasium.
- **Commands**: the Greek ones in `DATA/COMMANDS/GREEK18.XML`; what the 1.8 added to the game's command files (a Greek barracks source, a research…) is a patch that adds only that `<src>`.
- **Game files it edited**: Greek behavior in `ONFINISH_RESEARCH.vs` and `UNIT_ON_KILL.vs` (Supplies, Charm of Athena, Charm of Aegle, Statue of Zeus), the Greek items and formations, and the Greek hero names are **patches** that add; hero names are `QName00x` in `const.ini`.
- **AI variables**: `AIV_MaxQHoplite`, `AIV_MaxQMyrmidon`… (per difficulty) and `AIV_TimeStrenghtenStrongholdGates` are declared in the `AI.INI` patch. A script that reads one that is not declared does not compile.
- **AI recruiting**: `extras/greek-mod-1-8/patches/DATA/AI/TSH_RECRUITARMY.vs` (by hand): a Greek settlement asks for `trainQHoplite`… and the Greek researches; every other civilization takes the game's branch. It is a patch because the game calls this helper from its tactics, not through `AIRun`.
- **AI**: the 11 helper scripts and the main loop (`ES_Stronghold`) the mod rewrote for "Greece = Imperial Rome" run as `GR18_*` for Greek settlements only: a patch at the top of each game helper hands a Greek settlement over with `AIRun` (as the CGWAR does); `ES_Stronghold`, which `Sleep()`s and crashes inside `AIRun`, goes in `[EconomyScripts]` and `GetEconomyScript.vs` picks it. Every other civilization keeps HD's AI.
- **Interface**: `INFOBAR_GREEK18.INI`, `CMDBARGREEK18.INI` and the frame in `FRAMES.INI` (a patch) are what the game looks up by `internalName`.
- **Maps** (`Scenarios`, `Adventures`, `Conquests`, `ConquestMaps`, `Movies`: 124 `.bfhp`): kept. 10 of them place Greek classes; they are rewritten inside the HPFS container (1111 names, one letter for another, in place), so they place `QHoplite`, not `HHoplite`. **Players of race 4 in the maps (reviewed 2026-10-25, nothing changed).** A map names its players' race in `player<N>.xml` (`race="ImperialRome"`), and in the 1.8 race 4 *was* Greece; now it is the game's Imperial Rome. Of the 124 maps, 35 have an `ImperialRome` slot among the lobby slots 0-7 (60 slots), but almost all place nothing Greek there (empty or `Disabled` slots, or the game's Imperial Rome proper: `MTownhall`, `MHero1`, as in *Tensione al confine*, *Great Losses*), and they play as before. The `Q*` classes owned by Gaul, Iberian, Egyptian... players in other maps (Greek mercenaries and allies, e.g. *Conquista della Gallia*, *ARRI' ARRI'*, *@MOD_2on2_Greece_Ibe*) are the same as in the 1.8 and need nothing. The two conquests that place a lot of `Q*` (`Conquests/mediterranean.BFHP`, `Conquests/ConquestRemake.bfhp`) are not a Greek player in a Roman slot in the map's files: a conquest has one battle map per territory (`Maps/3`...`Maps/11`), they all use the same root `player<N>.xml`, the defender is always player 2 (ally player 3, race `Egypt`) and what it owns is the territory's (`I*` in Spain, `B*` Britain, `R*` Rome, `Q*` Greece in `Maps/11`). So the race of a territory's player is not in the map: the game takes it from the territory. **Tried in game 2026-10-25 (`dev-villoh` DLL): choosing Hellas (`Greece`, index 9) in `mediterranean` the player appears as Rome** (in the 1.8 "Greece" was race 4; here an unknown territory ends up as `ImperialRome`). Nothing in a map file can fix it, and the DLL has no handling of `territories.xml`: asked for in imperivm-resurrected/imperivm-resurrected#44 (a `race` attribute on a territory). Until then the Greek territory plays as Rome. **Tried and taken out:** `race="greek18"` in a map's `player<N>.xml` (the DLL reads that attribute only to show it in the picker, `src/game/mapinfo.cpp`; the game reads the file with its eight names and nothing in the DLL hooks that) and renaming the `MWalls*`/`MTower`/`MGate*` those slots own to `Q*`. Neither is evidenced, so the maps are as `port-140.js` and the name swap leave them. **Open, needs a conquest played in game** (and the engine, if a map is to name a mod civilization at all: the same question for any map of any mod).
- **Menu skin** (`CURRENTLANG/*` and, by hand, `DATA/INTERFACE/MENU/MAINMENU.INI` and `TEMPLATE.INI`, 1920x1080): kept. It is the one thing that is a whole file at the game's path, on purpose: it is cosmetic and only one skin can show at a time. It is also what tells the player that the mod is loaded.
- **Random map**: the mod's own Greek towns and villages (`Packs/RandomMapSettlements.bfhp`) are `Greek Rattlesmake Town N` / `Village N`, with the renamed classes, as a patch of `randommapsettlements`.
- References to 24 scripts the mod never shipped (`hero_gives_double_items`, `return_range_unit`, `centaur_summoning`, `paeceful_idle`…) are removed from the classes: the game could not run them, and the validator reports them as errors.

## Languages (reviewed 2026-10-25)

The original ships `Local\{czech,english,italian,spanish}.pak`; `flatten-paks.js` unpacks only `spanish.pak` (the greater name wins, and `port-140.js` reads one table), so the port had only `loc/spanish.loc.xml`. Measured against HD's own tables (`originals/game-data/local-*`):

- **Italian** (`Local\italian.pak`): 213 entries of `GAME.LOC.XML` and 3 of `TRANSLATION.LOC.XML` differ from HD's Italian (205 new keys: the Greek names and descriptions). **Ported**: `tools/greek-1-8.js` (step 0b) writes `loc/italian.loc.xml` (215 entries, renamed with the rest of the text files) and `local/italian/CREDITS.TXT`, with the same rule as the Spanish file. The rest of the pak (`HELP.XML`, `TIPS.XML`, `SRCSTRINGS.XML`, `SPLASH.INI`, the voices) is HD's.
- **English**: 4 entries, none of Greek content (the keys are English), one with Italian text in an English table ("Scudo della nobiltà"). Not ported.
- **Czech**: a whole translation of the game (456 files, 823 voices, 94 MB), a language HD does not have; it would be a new language with `"language"` in `mod.json`, not part of the Greek Mod. Not ported.
- **Spanish** (237 entries) is the original translation, written by Italian speakers: it keeps Italian words (`Mirmidone`, "El mirmidone luchará con la espada"). Left as the mod has it.
- **Texts that change the game's own.** The mod's tables also change HD's texts for everybody (`Republican Rome@race` becomes "Romano" / "Roma", `Multiplayer` "Varios jugadores", `News` "Extras", unit-special descriptions...), against "the game's 8 are never modified" (AGENTS.md). `tools/greek-1-8-civs.js` now leaves them out of both files (6 in Spanish, which the first port had: the menu's `News` and `Multiplayer`; 9 in Italian) once the civilizations are cloned. When the key's context is the name of a command a civilization cloned (`investR`), the text is kept for each clone's command (5 in Italian: `investJ`, the Imperial and Punic ones...). Tried in game: no.

## What `tools/greek-1-8-civs.js` does: the game's civilizations as the mod changed them

One civilization per row of its table: `rome18` (Republican Rome, letter `J`), `gaul18` (`L`), `carthage18` (`P`), `britain18` (`O`), `iberia18` (`U`), `egypt18` (`V`), `germany18` (`F`); indices 33-39, **kept** (published numbers and prefixes stay; engine #37 does not need them changed). **`empire18` (Imperial Rome) and `punic18` (Punic Rome) are new: no `letter` and no `index` in the table, the prefix is derived from the id by the SDK (`Emp`, `Pun`) and the engine assigns the index.** The ids are not `imperial18`/`romepun18` on purpose: the first three characters are the prefix, and `Imp` is GBR Tactics' (`imperial_gbrt`) and `Rom` would be shared by both. It runs after `greek-1-8.js`, which keeps what `port-140.js` merged for the game's classes and commands (HD + the 1.8's changes) in `originals/greek-1-8-merged`. `node tools/greek-1-8-civs.js [id ...]` builds only the ones named (after a fresh `greek-1-8.js`).

- **Clone, from the mod's version.** The SDK's `clone-civilization.js` clones the base from a staged DATA: HD's classes and commands with the mod's version of the civilization's own classes and of its barracks commands over them. So the copy has the 1.8's stats and costs.
- **Base classes inside the copy.** What the 1.8 changed in `Ranged`, `Hero`, `Catapult`... (`base-changes.json`) is written inside each copy, as for Greece (50 elements for Rome). A copy whose parent is a copy has it through the parent.
- **The mod's own new classes that its maps place** (`RVillager_Apollonia`, `GVillager_Apollonia`...) become the copy's (`JVillager_Apollonia`) and the map is rewritten inside (HPFS), as for Greece.
- **Command files that are the civilization's alone** (every source one of its classes: `GBARRACKS`, `CBARRACKS`, `CTEMPLE`, `TBARRACKS`, `TTEMPLEOFNEPTUS`, `TTENT`) are staged too, so the copy has the 1.8's costs.
- **Not ported.** The Rome branch choice (`RHT_SCELTA_ROMA.XML`, the `R*_Enable_Or_Disable_Cmd.vs` behaviors: they are not in any class), references to scripts the mod never shipped (36).
- **The mod's `Hero<unit>` classes** (unmounted heroes for the editor: `HeroRVelit`...) become `Hero<letter><unit>` (`HeroJVelit`), with the civilization's race (the mod's `HeroRVelit` had `ImperialRome`: a mistake of the mod).
- **Rome's three branches** (2026-10-25). In the mod the player researched `ChooseRepublicanRome`, `ChooseImperialRome` or `ChoosePunicRome` at the town hall and six scripts (`RARENA`, `RBARRACKS`, `RBLACKSMITH`, `RTAVERN`, `RTEMPLE`, `RTOWNHALL` `_ENABLE_OR_DISABLE_CMD.VS`) hid what the other branches own. Each branch is a civilization that starts with its branch's commands: `tools/lib/branch.js` runs the scripts once with the branch's flag (the same reader the 4.2 uses; the scripts have the same names, `greek-1-8.js` does not ship them, so `greek-1-8-civs.js` copies them from `originals/greek-mod-1.8-flat` into the port while it clones and takes them out after). What each leaves out:
  - **Republican** (`rome18`, `J`, 33): no Imperial hero hire, `RGladiator Shows2`, Standartization I/II, Flexibility, Liberati, Military Academy, `trainMVelit`, `trainMPraetorian`, Roman Velit/Praetorian researches.
  - **Imperial** (`empire18`, `Emp`): no `hireheroR` (its hero is `ImperialRomanHero`, hired with `hireheroEmp` for 600), no `RGladiator Shows`, Rome Training 1-3, Veteran Offence/Defence/Health, `RNobility`, `trainRGladiator`, `trainRTribune`, Equip Chariot, Roman Full Armor / Chariot Wheels, `buyfoodforgold`. It has `trainMVelit`, `trainMPraetorian`, Liberati, Military Academy.
  - **Punic** (`punic18`, `Pun`): the Republican hero (`hireheroR`) and gladiators, plus Liberati, Military Academy and `trainMPraetorian`; no Imperial hero, Standartization/Flexibility, Veteran researches, `RNobility`, `trainMVelit`, Tribune, Chariot, Roman Velit / Full Armor / Chariot Wheels.
  - Town hall and temple: the scripts only enable there once a branch is chosen, so nothing is hidden (the mules, auto-train and military training are there from the start).
  - **Velit and Praetorian** are the game's Imperial Rome classes (`RVelit`, `RPraetorian`, race `ImperialRome`; the mod trained them in the Republican barracks). `asBase` in the table makes `clone-civs.js` clone them with the branch's race (`EmpVelit`, `EmpPraetorian`, `PunPraetorian`); untouched by the mod, `RPraetorian` is read from HD.
  - `ImperialRomanHero` (race `RepublicanRome` in the mod) is only in `empire18` (`EmpImperialRomanHero`); `rome18` had it before and does not any more (`skipExtra`).
- **`cloneAllOrders` is on** (it was off). The mod changed 12 orders that the clones shared with the base civilizations as plain patches (Rome Training 2, Warrior Tales, Spoils of war, Sacred Legion, Eagle Summoning, German Eagle Summoning, Britain Spears/Swords, Roman Principle/Praetorian/Full Armor/Chariot Wheels...): without the flag a clone offered HD's order, with the mod's costs and texts lost. With it every order of the clone's buildings is its own copy (`<order> <Id>`), so 0 shared commands are patched and the mod's version is the one in the game (`Rome Training 2 Rome18` costs and params are the mod's). **Validated, not played**; if an order does not show or work in game, turn it off in `tools/greek-1-8-civs.js` (`cloneAllOrders`) and rebuild.
- **Editor heroes** (`HeroJVelit`...) exist for `rome18` only, the first clone of Rome: the mod's own files are removed once the first clone has taken them (the 4.2's second and third Rome have none either).
- **Classes by civilization** (2026-10-25): `rome18` 70 classes, 51 commands; `empire18` 73, 47; `punic18` 71, 48.
- **SDK fix.** `clone-civilization.js` had the wrong class id for the generic hero of 6 civilizations (`RomeHero`, `ImperialHero`, `CarthageHero`, `IberiaHero`, `BritainHero`, `EgyptHero`): the hero stayed the base's and the copy's heroes played as the base. Fixed in the SDK.

Still pending for these civilizations: the winter variants of the buildings (`RBarracksWinter`...: the clone tool reads the top folder of `classes` only), the mod's maps (they place the game's `R*` classes), and `unit_specials.ini`/`skills.ini` (global).
