# Greek Civilization Mod 4.2 (Gabbo)

`ported/greek-civilization-mod/` = `shrink-mod.js` on the GameBanana 728851 zip (`originals/gamebanana/728851/`), then this folder copied over it:

```bash
node shrink-mod.js <unzipped 4.2> "<HD game>" ../community-mods/ported/greek-civilization-mod --id greek-civilization-mod --name "Greek Civilization Mod 4.2" --version 4.2 --category Civilizations \
  --exclude "mod/*,logs/*,update.xml,README.txt,LEGGIMI_GREEKMOD.txt,GREEKMOD_MANIFEST.json,imperivm-mod.ini"
```

- `mod.json`: the one in the zip, with the GameBanana category name (`Civilizations`), the port credited and `name` = `Greek Civilization Mod 4.2` (the version is part of the name: the room's list of civilization sets is named after it, next to `Greek Civilization Mod 1.8` and `CGWAR - Greek civilization Mod`; the `id` stays `greek-civilization-mod`, which is what maps `require`; `tools/greek-4-2.js` sets it too).
- `DATA/CLASSES/BASETAVERN.SC.XML`: the mod's, with the entity path HD itself has broken (`Buildings/Tavern/Tavern_as…`, there is no such folder) pointing to `GTavern`, like Imperivm Legacy's. Nothing else changed.

## What a Resurrected mod cannot carry

The zip also brings an engine hook (`winmm.dll`, which builds `gbr_GreekMod_*.exe` from the player's `gbr.exe`), `imperivm-mod.ini`, `config.ini` and `mod/` (options menu with autosave, language selector, windowed mode: Open Imperivm and Syncrash). A mod cannot ship executables, and Resurrected has its own `winmm.dll`, so those are **not** ported: units, buildings, classes, AI scripts, texts, voices, sounds, music, terrains, graphics and the 2.5-based scenarios/adventures are.

## Greece in the lobby (civs/greek.json)

In 4.2 the Greek faction sits in the Imperial Rome slot: its classes have `race="ImperialRome"` and the letter `H` (`HTownhall`, `HHoplite`), the scripts say `Greece = ImperialRome`, and Gabbo's engine hook shows it in the lobby. Here Greece is its own civilization, `civs/greek.json` (`greek`, letter `H`, index 31, race `greek`, `template`, `ai: true`, no `replacesBase`): in no one's slot, Imperial Rome is the game's again. The port's own Imperial Rome is `romeimp42`. The city and villages are "Greek Gabbo Town N" / "Greek Gabbo Village N", templates added by a patch (the mod's `Packs/RandomMapSettlements.bfhp` is not shipped).

## Game modes (tools/greek-mod-fixes.js)

4.2 replaced the game's five victory scripts with nine of its own (`1 VARIOS` … `9 CUSTOM`), kept its edited copies of the old five inside `data.pak` as `.VX` (nothing reads them) and its old pack hid the game's. Most maps say `victory_condition="1 Elimination"` (228 of the 415 maps in `ported/`), so with that script hidden a map starts and goes back to the menu at once, with nothing in the log. `shrink-mod.js` carries over the hiding; `node tools/greek-mod-fixes.js` takes the five out of `hidden.txt` (the game's own scripts, as the maps were made for) and drops the `.VX`. The lobby lists them next to 4.2's nine.

## Independence (tools/greek-4-2.js, AGENTS.md)

`tools/greek-4-2.js` rebuilds `ported/greek-civilization-mod` from `originals/greek-4-2-pristine` (the port as shrink-mod, the copy of this folder and `greek-mod-fixes.js` leave it; hard links) and makes **every civilization its own**:

- **The game's classes** the mod changed are cloned from HD with the mod's version on top (`tools/lib/clone-civs.js`, the SDK's `clone-civilization.js --all-orders`), and what it changed in the base classes (`Hero`, `Melee`...) is written inside each copy: Gaul `gaul42` (X), Britain `britain42` (Y), Iberia `iberia42` (Z), Egypt `egypt42` (1), Germany `germany42` (3). The mod's own new classes of those races (heroes, Apollonia villagers...) are copies too.
- **Rome and Carthage**: the mod let the player pick the branch at the town hall (`ChooseXxx`), per-building scripts hid what the other branches own. Each branch is a civilization with only the commands its branch left visible (`tools/lib/branch.js` reads the scripts): Republican `romerep42` (4), Imperial `romeimp42` (5, hero `ImperialRomanHero`), Punic `romepun42` (6, `AncientRomanHero`), Numidian `carthnum42` (7), Mauritanian `carthmau42` (8, `MauritanianHero`). Nothing at a town hall offers to change it. The "Imperivm 2" old-school orders (`ReqModeImp2`) are not in them.
- **Greece** (`greek`, H, index 31): its classes have race `greek`, in no one's slot; its walls, gates and tower (the mod drew them over Imperial Rome's ids) are `H*` classes; its AI scripts run as `GR42_*`; its random map towns are "Greek Gabbo Town N"; its hero names are `HName00x`.
- **The game's files** the mod replaced: only what is Greek stays, as patches (Olympic Games, the kill bonuses by hand in `extras/greek-4-2-hand/`, the counters, items, outposts of the random map, the editor icons); the rest, `hidden.txt` and the game packs (`Packs/*.bfhp`) are left out (`raw/greek-4-2-left-out.txt`). The menu skin: images stay at the game's paths (the content manager lets the player choose), its layout is a patch of the keys it changed.
- **Names**: what the CGWAR or the 1.8 also has with other bytes (classes, art folders, icons, researches, items, maps) is renamed `_g42` / `(g42)`; the mod's versions of the game's official adventures stay, with "(Gabbo)" in their title.

Tried in game (2026-10-23, Linux, 1920x1080): the lobby with the three Greek mods active (all the civilizations show, no patch errors, the content manager shows no problem), a skirmish with several civilizations that hung because of script patches of the CGWAR that did not compile (fixed, see its NOTES). **Not tried**: a game with every civilization of the 4.2, the Greek AI, the Roman branches' barracks, heroes.

Left out, to port later: the game modes' rules for the clones (see *Game modes*), the AI scripts the engine calls by name (see *The mod's AI*), `Spartacus`, `Fisherman`... (neutral, renamed).

## The mod's versions of the game's scripts (tools/lib/own-scripts.js)

The clones and Greece played in the 4.2 with the mod's edits of the game's behavior scripts (`barrack_train.vs`, `hero_attack.vs`, `druid_learn.vs`, the blacksmith and temple animations, the catapults, the village and outpost scripts...). They used to be left out (747 files of the mod's at the game's paths; `raw/greek-4-2-left-out.txt`) because the game's scripts must not change for the game's civilizations. Now each one the port's classes and commands name (and the ones those start) is a copy of its own, `<name>_g42.vs`, and only the port's classes and commands point to it: 112 copies, listed in `raw/greek-4-2-own-scripts.txt`. The game's scripts stay the game's.

- **The four that read the branch flags** (`onfinish_research.vs`, `arena_hirehero.vs`, `verify_research.vs`, `specialize_build_verify.vs`) are copied too, with the flags **written into the copy** as true or false at build time (nothing is chosen at run time): all false for every civilization except the two Carthages (Numidian: `ChooseNumidianCarthage` true; Mauritanian: its own), so Rome's branches get the `heroClass` path of the order (their hero comes in the order's parameter, as in the game's script) and `verify_research`'s check of the branch is gone. Ids the script writes as text are the civilization's own where it matters: the shaman of Dark Rituals (`7Shaman`/`7ShamanNumidian`, `8Shaman`/`8ShamanNumidian`) and Britain's bet of Fights (`Britain42 Fights`). 124 copies in all (`raw/greek-4-2-own-scripts.txt`), a civilization whose text equals the rest shares the copy. What is still the game's or not mapped: the unit ids `specialize_build_verify.vs` compares (`CMauritanianWrestler`...) are the mod's old ids, not the clones', so its filtering finds no match.
- **Class ids written as text** in a shared script (`Place("EPriest")`) are the game's or the mod's old ids, not the clone's: they place the game's class, as the game's script did. Where it matters they are mapped per civilization (the script is copied once per civilization): the shaman of Dark Rituals, Britain's Fights, and the mercenary of Carthage's outpost (`CMERCENARIO` -> `7MERCENARIO` / `8MERCENARIO`). Not mapped: `BHorseman` of the village script (Britain's branch of `iberian_village_trainIScout.vs`): that class does not exist in the game nor in the mod, the mod's own script misspells it.
- **const.ini**: the keys the game does not have (23: `MaxMyrmidonsValue`, `Stonehenge*`, `AntiRonda*`, `LastRoarRadius`, `DesertSpiritsRadius`, `RaidsTimes`, `RaidsFood`, `RaidsDuration`, the Carthage outpost's) are in a patch now: only the mod's scripts read them, and without them `GetConst` of the Myrmidons' school and the Stonehenge spells found nothing. A key the game has keeps the game's value.
- **`StaminaBoostSound`** (a feedback class nobody declares) is `StaminaBoost` in every script of the port. The Greek priest names `druid_revitalize*_g42.vs`.

## The mod's AI (tools/lib/greek-ai.js)

The AI of the mod runs only for the mod's civilizations: every script is a copy of its own (`GR42_*`) and the game's AI scripts are only handed over by patches that ask `Mod_TrueRace(set.GetCentralBuilding)`, so the game's civilizations keep the game's AI.

- **Economy and helpers** (`GR42_ES_Stronghold`, `GR42_ES_Village`, `GR42_ESH_*`, 12 scripts): for Greece, Gaul, Britain, Iberia, Egypt and Germany (`greek`, `gaul42`, `britain42`, `iberia42`, `egypt42`, `germany42`). They used to be Greece's only. Not for Rome's three branches and Carthage's two: those scripts read the old `ChooseXxx` flags, which no longer exist, so those five keep the game's economy.
- **Tactics** (new): the mod's `GetTacticScript` for each civilization, as the SDK says to give a civilization its own tactic (`sdk/civilizations.md`, *The AI*). `GR42_TS_*` (13 scripts, with `TS_AttackAtWill` they start) are declared in `AI.INI` and a patch of `GetTacticScript.vs` picks them in the first 2 seconds as the mod did: Greece, Gaul, Britain, Iberia, Germany 30% the first and 60% the second, Egypt 100%, Numidian Carthage 100% `TS_CarthageTactic`; Gaul none in Old School mode; Rome's three branches and the Mauritanian Carthage none (`TS_NONE`), as the mod gives them.
- **AI variables**: only the ones the game does not have are declared (`AIV_Max*` of the mod's units, `AIV_WaitTactic`...). Before, the patch also set `AIV_MaxMilUnits` (game: -1, mod: 120) and `AIV_MaxAttentionPoints` on Hard for **everybody**: gone, a variable the game has keeps its value.
- **Not copied: what the engine calls by name** (`CalcGAIKAPriority`, `GetArmyNeed`, `EvalRecruit`, `SendSquad`, `CalcMaxTake`, the `TSH_*` helpers behind `set.TSRecruitHero()`...), the group scripts (`GS_*`), `SquadMonitor`, `Recruiter`, `Main` and the `AI HELPERS` (siege, guard): there is one of each for all the AI players and the engine looks for it by that name, so a copy would not be called and replacing it would change every AI. The mod's versions of these are the game's here. Its tactics were written for them, so how closely the clones' AI plays like the 4.2's is **not tried**.
- **Tried in game** (2026-10-25, profile with only this mod, after the scripts and the AI above): the maintainer played and reported that it works ("parece que funciona bien"). Not itemised: which civilizations, how closely the AI plays like the 4.2's, the log was not read line by line here.

## Game modes (tools/greek-4-2.js, 3c)

The mod's nine modes (`1 VARIOS` ... `9 CUSTOM`) are modes of this mod: what they do to a civilization is for the mod's civilizations only. What touched the game's civilizations (found by reading what each script disables and on which class):

- **`1 Varios`** → `mode_varios_disable_upgrades.vs`: disabled Eagle/Wolf Summoning, Scout Area, Expedition and Gossip (all seven orders exist in the game) in the game's `RTavern`, `RTavern2`, `MTavern`, `MTavern2`, `CTavern`, `BTempleOfThor`, `EMarket` and `TTempleOfNeptus`.
- **`5 Old school`** (Imperivm 2): `mode_imperivm2_costs/rome/gaul/iberia.vs` and `mode_inverno_rules.vs` disabled 50+ of the game's orders in 22 of the game's buildings, and the "Imp2 Cost" orders that replaced them are not shipped, so a game Carthage lost its maceman for good. It also let the game's Rome, Gaul, Iberia and Carthage in.
- `mode_hide_imperivm2_costs.vs` (Varios, Liga, Punic, Custom...) names 22 of the game's classes but its 51 orders do not exist in the game: no effect.
- The Liga gates (`rtemple_liga_gate_*`) are attached only to the Roman clones' classes.
- Starting resources (`bldForce.race == Germany`, 8 modes) is the same rule as the game's.

Now Varios' rule is asked of the mod's civilizations only, and Old school lets in only Gaul 4.2, Iberia 4.2, Imperial Rome 4.2 and Numidian Carthage (the mod's four equivalents); every other player, the game's civilizations included, is out as the mode already did with the rest. **What is not done**: those rules name the game's classes and orders (`ClassPlayerObjs("CBarracks")`, "Barrack Level 2 Imp2 Cost"...), so for the mod's civilizations they find nothing: Imperivm 2's costs and kits, and Varios' hiding of the Liga upgrades, are not applied to the clones. **Tried in game** (2026-10-26, profile with only this mod): `5 Old school` eliminates a civilization that is not one of the four (Mauritanian Carthage, with the new message) and lets in Gaul 4.2 against Iberia 4.2; `1 Varios` starts and runs (random map, size 1, 6 positions, several games, no script errors in the log). One match of `1 Varios` on size 2 with 8 positions and random civilizations (Egypt 4.2, Britain 4.2, Iberia 4.2, three Imperial Romes 4.2, Numidian Carthage) closed the game 3 s after the start with an access violation inside `WINMM.dll` (the engine: a function that asks a game object of class `*Outpost*`/`*Fort*` for its position and finds it freed; `Logs/nixos-*/vx.log`); it did not come back in the next games. Not reproduced, not explained. Doing it means a script per civilization with the clone's class and order names (the clone tool's rename table is not exported) and the ~170 "Imp2 Cost" orders cloned for each of them.

## Rome's branches and Carthage's (tools/greek-4-2.js, tools/greek-mod-fixes.js)

In 4.2 Rome (Republican, Imperial, Punic) and Carthage (Numidian, Mauritanian) are picked before the lobby: Gabbo's engine hook leaves the choice in the sentinel `rand(776 + player)`, which five scripts read to research the branch's `ChooseXxx` at the start. Here each branch is a civilization of its own (`romerep42`, `romeimp42`, `romepun42`, `carthnum42`, `carthmau42`, indices 45-49), cloned from HD with its branch's commands already in its classes; nothing at a town hall chooses or switches it, and the sentinel and the scripts that read it (the `R*/C*_ENABLE_OR_DISABLE_CMD.VS`) are not shipped. The old `civs/rome*.json`/`carthage*.json` variants of `extras/` are removed by `greek-4-2.js`.

`greek-mod-fixes.js` still rewrites those five scripts (they feed `greek-4-2-pristine`); the final port does not carry them.

The same script gives the renamed classes their old HD id as `altid` (HD misspells `TValkyria`, `CMacemen`, `BHighlandar`, `IDefendar`, `GTridentWarriar`, `BaseBoilding`; 4.2 fixes them and drops the old ones, so HD's scripts and maps could not create them).

## 00_Ludus_Martis (the Community Mod's build, tools/greek-mod-fixes.js)

The map is Ferónidas's ("[CM] Ludus Martis"): the Community Mod v12 ships it (1,370,141 bytes) and it plays there. 4.2's package carries another build of it (1,369,117 bytes, adapted by someone: it swaps classes only the Community Mod has for the game's, translates the description and adds items and two sequences). That build has summon items whose scripts place `cRScout56` (and `cGHorseman56`, `cICavalry56`, `cCNumidianRider56`, `cTTeutonRider56`, `cTTeutonWolf56`, `cTValkyrie56`), classes no mod we have defines (4.2, Community Mod v12, Caesar Mod: not there); the game logs `No matching function with name cRScout56` and goes back to the menu one second after starting, also with 4.2's package installed unchanged as a mod (GameBanana 728851), so it is not the port. Seven copies of the game's horses under those ids removed the log errors but not the exit.

So the port ships the Community Mod's own build instead, unmodified, from its v12 package, plus the three hero classes it needs and the game does not have (`CHero5` Candecar, `EHero4` Menhotep, `BHero4` Calgacos; a few lines each over a game hero's entity). Tried in game with the Greek Mod: it works.

## Conquests (tools/greek-4-2.js, tools/lib/conquests.js)

The mod ships two conquests. Both collide by path (AGENTS.md, *Conquests and the engine*), so `greek-4-2.js` runs `lib/conquests.js` before `tagCollisions`:

- **Mediterranean** (`Conquests/mediterranean (g42).BFHP`, menu name "Forgia il tuo impero (Gabbo)"): next to the game's and the 1.8's. Its `ConquestMaps/1 - GBR Europe (g42)` is the game's folder under the new name (the mod ships none: its files are the game's). Its Greece territory has `interface="31"`, the index of `greek`, so it plays as the mod's Greece (it was 4, ImperialRome). **Needs a DLL with imperivm-resurrected#45**; with the 1.0 release the value is ignored and the player keeps the room's race. The mod declared the Greece territory (index 9) on the game's map, which has no region 9 and no `ShieldGreece.bmp`: Greece could not be clicked, also in the original 4.2. `tools/lib/conquest-greece.js` draws it in the copy of the folder: region 9 in `territories.bmp` (the Greek peninsula, the Aegean islands and Crete, found in `global.bmp` by colour inside a polygon, only on pixels that belong to nobody; ~7,800 px), the name HELLAS written on the peninsula in `global.bmp` as the other labels are (the territory's name in the mod's texts is "Hellas"), and `ShieldGreece.bmp`. The art is in `extras/greek-civilization-mod/conquest/`: `hellas-shield.png` is the bronze aspis of *Aspis hoplite shield* by ninelionar ([OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/aspis-hoplite-shield), CC0: render `aspis shield front 3D rendering.jpg`, cut out as a disk, with the name in Cinzel, OFL, below it) and `hellas-label.png` is the name in the same font, cream with a dark outline. **Tried in game** (2026-10-25, Linux): the conquest list, the map with Greece clickable and its text (Hellas); after the label and the shield were redone the maintainer reported it works.
- **L'unificazione italica** (Giuseppe D'Addio): `ConquestMaps/8 - GBR Italia (g42)`, "(Gabbo)" in the menu. Its territories are Italian peoples on the game's races (none is Greece): nothing to set. The same folder name is also in `ported/l-unificazione-italica` (a wrapped map that `requires` this mod and ships only its `territories.xml`); that is why this one is renamed. They are two different things and both stay: the wrapped map is the standalone one, this conquest is the one that comes with the 4.2.

## Review against the new engine (imperivm-resurrected#37 and #38, `dev-villoh`)

- **Prefixes and indices (#37)**: kept. The 11 civilizations keep their one-character letters and explicit indices (31, 40-49): published numbers stay as they are, and changing a letter renames every class of the mod. `hirehero5`, `hirehero6`, `hirehero8`... are command ids, not prefixes. This task adds no civilization, so nothing needed a derived prefix.
- **Set name (#38)**: the set is named after `name` in `mod.json`: `Greek Civilization Mod 4.2` (it was `Greek Civilization Mod`). The `id` does not change. Nothing else carried the old name: `compare-mods.js` does not print it, maps `require` the id, the dropdown labels are "<civ> (Gabbo)" (Gabbo is the author in `mod.json` too, same as the set). The registry `sdk/prefixes.md` of `imperivm-resurrected` still lists the pack as "Greek Civilization Mod" (not this repository).
- **A room with only this set**: all 11 civilizations have `ai: true`, none has `random: false`, none uses `replacesBase`, `variant` or `ownAi`, so the dropdowns, the random draw and the AI have something to pick (read from `civs/*.json`; the set shown in a room: not itemised in the maintainer's test).
- **What assumes it is always in the room**: the Greek AI (`GR42_*`, `patches/DATA/AI/`) and `HOUTPOST_BEHAVIOR.VS` ask `Mod_TrueRace(...) == Greek`, the race of this mod's Greece: they work whenever the mod is loaded, whatever set the room allows. No script asks `Mod_PlayerCiv` of an id of another mod, and none of this mod's civilizations needs another mod to be in the room. A map that `requires` the mod places its `H*` classes whatever the room allows (the engine's rule, **not tried here**).
