# Greek Civilization Mod 4.2 (Gabbo)

`ported/greek-civilization-mod/` = `tools/greek-4-2.js` on `originals/greek-4-2-pristine-731819`, which `tools/greek-4-2-source.js` makes from Gabbo's package of 26 October 2026 (GameBanana 731819, `originals/gamebanana/731819/greekmod_42_completa_20261026_c205a.zip`, MD5 `442a4c8e430e97e07a99972e4eb02f75`):

```bash
node tools/greek-4-2-source.js --force      # needs G (the HD game folder) for the music, and originals/greek-4-2-pristine (the old source) for 3 sounds
node tools/greek-4-2.js
```

Both are deterministic (two runs give the same 4,380 files, byte for byte). The old chain (`shrink-mod.js` on the 728851 zip, `greek-mod-fixes.js`) made `originals/greek-4-2-pristine`; that page is private now and the folder is only the source of three sounds (below). `greek-mod-fixes.js` still runs, from `greek-4-2-source.js`, on the new folder.

- `mod.json`: ours (`extras/`), with the GameBanana category name (`Civilizations`), the port credited and `name` = `Greek Civilization Mod 4.2` (the version is part of the name: the room's list of civilization sets is named after it, next to `Greek Civilization Mod 1.8` and `CGWAR - Greek civilization Mod`; the `id` stays `greek-civilization-mod`, which is what maps `require`; `tools/greek-4-2.js` sets it too), `version` the package's (`4.2.20261026.1`), the page 731819 as the source.
- `Licenses/`: `CREDITS.txt` (the credits the package publishes) and the Caesar Mod's CC BY-SA 4.0 (see *The package of 26 October 2026*).
- `DATA/CLASSES/BASETAVERN.SC.XML` (the old fix of an entity path HD has broken) is no longer shipped: the package sets `entity=""` there and validates.

## What a Resurrected mod cannot carry

The old zip brought an engine hook (`winmm.dll`, which builds `gbr_GreekMod_*.exe` from the player's `gbr.exe`), `imperivm-mod.ini`, `config.ini` and `mod/` (options menu with autosave, language selector, windowed mode: Open Imperivm and Syncrash); the 26 October package brings a fork of Resurrected 1.0.0 instead (`winmm.dll`, `steam_api.dll`, an Extra menu, the editor's free play, automatic updates off). A mod cannot ship executables, and Resurrected has its own `winmm.dll`, so those are **not** ported: units, buildings, classes, AI scripts, texts, voices, sounds, music, terrains, graphics and the 2.5-based scenarios/adventures are.

## Greece in the lobby (civs/greek.json)

In 4.2 the Greek faction sits in the Imperial Rome slot: its classes have `race="ImperialRome"` and the letter `H` (`HTownhall`, `HHoplite`), the scripts say `Greece = ImperialRome`, and Gabbo's engine hook shows it in the lobby. Here Greece is its own civilization, `civs/greek.json` (`greek`, letter `H`, index 31, race `greek`, `template`, `ai: true`, no `replacesBase`): in no one's slot, Imperial Rome is the game's again. The port's own Imperial Rome is `romeimp42`. The city and villages are "Greek Gabbo Town N" / "Greek Gabbo Village N", templates added by a patch (the mod's `Packs/RandomMapSettlements.bfhp` is not shipped).

## Game modes (tools/greek-mod-fixes.js)

4.2 replaced the game's five victory scripts with nine of its own (`1 VARIOS` … `9 CUSTOM`), kept its edited copies of the old five inside `data.pak` as `.VX` (nothing reads them) and its old pack hid the game's. Most maps say `victory_condition="1 Elimination"` (228 of the 415 maps in `ported/`), so with that script hidden a map starts and goes back to the menu at once, with nothing in the log. `shrink-mod.js` carries over the hiding; `node tools/greek-mod-fixes.js` takes the five out of `hidden.txt` (the game's own scripts, as the maps were made for) and drops the `.VX`. The lobby lists them next to 4.2's nine.

## Independence (tools/greek-4-2.js, AGENTS.md)

`tools/greek-4-2.js` rebuilds `ported/greek-civilization-mod` from `originals/greek-4-2-pristine-731819` (hard links) and makes **every civilization its own**:

- **The game's classes** the mod changed are cloned from HD with the mod's version on top (`tools/lib/clone-civs.js`, the SDK's `clone-civilization.js --all-orders`), and what it changed in the base classes (`Hero`, `Melee`...) is written inside each copy: Gaul `gaul42` (X), Britain `britain42` (Y), Iberia `iberia42` (Z), Egypt `egypt42` (1), Germany `germany42` (3). The mod's own new classes of those races (heroes, Apollonia villagers...) are copies too.
- **Rome and Carthage**: the mod let the player pick the branch at the town hall (`ChooseXxx`), per-building scripts hid what the other branches own. Each branch is a civilization with only the commands its branch left visible (`tools/lib/branch.js` reads the scripts): Republican `romerep42` (4), Imperial `romeimp42` (5, hero `ImperialRomanHero`), Punic `romepun42` (6, `AncientRomanHero`), Numidian `carthnum42` (7), Mauritanian `carthmau42` (8, `MauritanianHero`). Nothing at a town hall offers to change it. The "Imperivm 2" old-school orders (`ReqModeImp2`) are not in them.
- **Greece** (`greek`, H, index 31): its classes have race `greek`, in no one's slot; its walls, gates and tower (the mod drew them over Imperial Rome's ids) are `H*` classes; its AI scripts run as `GR42_*`; its random map towns are "Greek Gabbo Town N"; its hero names are `HName00x`.
- **The game's files** the mod replaced: only what is Greek stays, as patches (Olympic Games, the kill bonuses by hand in `extras/greek-4-2-hand/`, the counters, items, outposts of the random map, the editor icons); the rest, `hidden.txt` and the game packs (`Packs/*.bfhp`) are left out (`raw/greek-4-2-left-out.txt`). The menu skin: images stay at the game's paths (the content manager lets the player choose), its layout is a patch of the keys it changed.
- **Names**: what the CGWAR or the 1.8 also has with other bytes (classes, art folders, icons, researches, items, maps) is renamed `_g42` / `(g42)`; the mod's versions of the game's official adventures stay, with "(Gabbo)" in their title.

Tried in game (2026-10-23, Linux, 1920x1080): the lobby with the three Greek mods active (all the civilizations show, no patch errors, the content manager shows no problem), a skirmish with several civilizations that hung because of script patches of the CGWAR that did not compile (fixed, see its NOTES). **Not tried**: a game with every civilization of the 4.2, the Greek AI, the Roman branches' barracks, heroes.

Left out: the AI the engine calls with no player (the `AIO*` orders and the siege and guard helpers, see *The mod's AI*). `Spartacus` and `Fisherman`... (neutral, renamed) stay.

## The mod's versions of the game's scripts (tools/lib/own-scripts.js)

The clones and Greece played in the 4.2 with the mod's edits of the game's behavior scripts (`barrack_train.vs`, `hero_attack.vs`, `druid_learn.vs`, the blacksmith and temple animations, the catapults, the village and outpost scripts...). They used to be left out (747 files of the mod's at the game's paths; `raw/greek-4-2-left-out.txt`) because the game's scripts must not change for the game's civilizations. Now each one the port's classes and commands name (and the ones those start) is a copy of its own, `<name>_g42.vs`, and only the port's classes and commands point to it: 250 files (a civilization whose text differs from the rest gets its own copy, `<name>_g42_<civ id>.vs`), listed in `raw/greek-4-2-own-scripts.txt`. The game's scripts stay the game's.

- **Methods a class inherits from the game's classes** (`Unit`'s attack, `Military`'s guardengage): nothing of the port names those scripts, so the copies above never reached the clones. For the scripts the 26 October package edited that way (`unit_attack`, `unit_engage`, `unit_engage_unit_type`, `unit_engage_unit_type_exclusive`, `unit_guard_engage`, `unit_capture`: the archers' flight distance and the capture of ambient peasants) `own-scripts.js` writes the `<method>` elements of the nearest game ancestor inside each class of the port (all the `<method>` of that `sig`, as the game restates a whole sig when it redefines one), pointing to the copy: 2,049 elements in 478 classes. Behaviors a class inherits are not overridden (writing one inside would run both): `arena_behavior`, `gate_patrol`, `hero_skill_behaviour`, `outpost_behavior`, `townhall_behavior_guard`, `settlement_behavior_ambient`, `townhall_sentries_control`, `village_behavior_givefood`, `wall_patrol`, `magic_well_behavior`, `summoning_behavior` stay the game's. The other 49 inherited scripts the mod edited in older versions are **not** written inside (`inherit` lists the ones to do); they were never in the port and nobody has asked for them.

- **The four that read the branch flags** (`onfinish_research.vs`, `arena_hirehero.vs`, `verify_research.vs`, `specialize_build_verify.vs`) are copied too, with the flags **written into the copy** as true or false at build time (nothing is chosen at run time): all false for every civilization except the two Carthages (Numidian: `ChooseNumidianCarthage` true; Mauritanian: its own), so Rome's branches get the `heroClass` path of the order (their hero comes in the order's parameter, as in the game's script) and `verify_research`'s check of the branch is gone. Ids the script writes as text are the civilization's own where it matters: the shaman of Dark Rituals (`7Shaman`/`7ShamanNumidian`, `8Shaman`/`8ShamanNumidian`) and Britain's bet of Fights (`Britain42 Fights`). A civilization whose text equals the rest shares the copy.
- **Class ids written as text** (`Place("EPriest", ...)`, `.class == "GArcher"`, the units `specialize_build_verify.vs` compares): in the copy for each clone every literal that is a class id of the game or of the mod, and has a clone (`EPriest` -> `1Priest`, `CMauritanianWrestler` -> `7MauritanianWrestler`, `NSECUAZ` -> `7NSECUAZ`), is written as the clone's, so the script places and tests the civilization's own classes (78 per-civilization copies). A literal of another civilization's class stays as it is (a script that lists every race keeps its dispatch). Also per civilization: the shaman of Dark Rituals, Britain's Fights, and the mercenary of Carthage's outpost. Not mapped: `BHorseman` in `iberian_village_trainIScout.vs` (its Britain branch): that class does not exist in the game nor in the mod, the mod's own script misspells it, so there is nothing to map it to.
- **const.ini**: the keys the game does not have (23: `MaxMyrmidonsValue`, `Stonehenge*`, `AntiRonda*`, `LastRoarRadius`, `DesertSpiritsRadius`, `RaidsTimes`, `RaidsFood`, `RaidsDuration`, the Carthage outpost's) are in a patch now: only the mod's scripts read them, and without them `GetConst` of the Myrmidons' school and the Stonehenge spells found nothing. A key the game has keeps the game's value.
- **`StaminaBoostSound`** (a feedback class nobody declares) is `StaminaBoost` in every script of the port. The Greek priest names `druid_revitalize*_g42.vs`.

## The mod's AI (tools/lib/greek-ai.js)

The AI of the mod runs only for the mod's civilizations: every script is a copy of its own (`GR42_*`) and the game's AI scripts are only handed over by patches that ask `Mod_TrueRace(set.GetCentralBuilding)`, so the game's civilizations keep the game's AI.

- **Economy and helpers** (`GR42_ES_Stronghold`, `GR42_ES_Village`, `GR42_ESH_*`, 12 scripts): for Greece, Gaul, Britain, Iberia, Egypt and Germany (`greek`, `gaul42`, `britain42`, `iberia42`, `egypt42`, `germany42`). They used to be Greece's only. Not for Rome's three branches and Carthage's two: those scripts read the old `ChooseXxx` flags, which no longer exist, so those five keep the game's economy.
- **Tactics** (new): the mod's `GetTacticScript` for each civilization, as the SDK says to give a civilization its own tactic (`sdk/civilizations.md`, *The AI*). `GR42_TS_*` (13 scripts, with `TS_AttackAtWill` they start) are declared in `AI.INI` and a patch of `GetTacticScript.vs` picks them in the first 2 seconds as the mod did: Greece, Gaul, Britain, Iberia, Germany 30% the first and 60% the second, Egypt 100%, Numidian Carthage 100% `TS_CarthageTactic`; Gaul none in Old School mode; Rome's three branches and the Mauritanian Carthage none (`TS_NONE`), as the mod gives them.
- **AI variables**: only the ones the game does not have are declared (`AIV_Max*` of the mod's units, `AIV_WaitTactic`...). Before, the patch also set `AIV_MaxMilUnits` (game: -1, mod: 120) and `AIV_MaxAttentionPoints` on Hard for **everybody**: gone, a variable the game has keeps its value.
- **The rest of the mod's AI** (`tools/lib/own-ai.js`):
  - **The monitors** `Main.vs` starts (`Prioritize`, `Recruiter`, `GAIKAMonitor`, `SquadMonitor`, `EconomyMonitor`, `TacticMonitor`) and what they start (the group strategies `GS_*`, the helpers `GSH_*`) are `GR42_*` copies, declared in `AI.INI` (the strategies in `[GAIKAStrat]`, with the names of the copies written into each other). A patch of `Main.vs` starts them, and returns, for the AI players of the mod's civilizations (`Mod_PlayerCiv(AIGetPlayer)`, as the CGWAR does for its squad monitor). `GS_Guard` is not the mod's: it stays the game's.
  - **What the engine calls by name and is not started by a script** (`CalcGAIKAPriority`, `EvalRecruit`, `CalcMaxTake`, `CheckMAIKA`, `GetGAIKAStrat`, `HeroSkill Default`, `TSH_HeroSkills`, `TSH_Research`, `TSH_TempleRecruit`, and for the civilizations whose AI does not read the old branch flags `TSH_RecruitArmy`, `TSH_HeroRecruit`, `TSH_ArenaRecruit`): there is one for every AI player and no script can call another one (`AIRun` starts it, it does not return its value), so the mod's body goes inside the game's, in a block `if (Mod_PlayerCiv(<player>) == ...)` (a patch, `@before` the game's first line of code, after its declarations). **Not tried in game: a compilation error in one of these patches would stop the game's own script for every AI player**, not only the mod's: read `imperivm-mod.log`.
  - **Left the game's**: the `AIO*` orders (`AIOSendSquad`, `AIOTrain`, `AIOSupplySet/Squad`, `AIOUpgradeSet`) and the `AI HELPERS` (siege and guard of a group), which the engine calls with no player or civilization in their parameters (set index, group name): there is nothing to ask. Rome's three branches and Carthage's two use the game's `TSH_RecruitArmy`, `TSH_HeroRecruit` and `TSH_ArenaRecruit` and economy (their mod versions read the old `ChooseXxx` flags).
- **Tried in game** (2026-10-25, profile with only this mod, after the scripts and the AI above): the maintainer played and reported that it works ("parece que funciona bien"). Not itemised: which civilizations, how closely the AI plays like the 4.2's, the log was not read line by line here.

## Game modes (tools/greek-4-2.js, 3c)

The mod's nine modes (`1 VARIOS` ... `9 CUSTOM`) are modes of this mod: what they do to a civilization is for the mod's civilizations only. What touched the game's civilizations (found by reading what each script disables and on which class):

- **`1 Varios`** → `mode_varios_disable_upgrades.vs`: disabled Eagle/Wolf Summoning, Scout Area, Expedition and Gossip (all seven orders exist in the game) in the game's `RTavern`, `RTavern2`, `MTavern`, `MTavern2`, `CTavern`, `BTempleOfThor`, `EMarket` and `TTempleOfNeptus`.
- **`5 Old school`** (Imperivm 2): `mode_imperivm2_costs/rome/gaul/iberia.vs` and `mode_inverno_rules.vs` disabled 50+ of the game's orders in 22 of the game's buildings, and the "Imp2 Cost" orders that replaced them are not shipped, so a game Carthage lost its maceman for good. It also let the game's Rome, Gaul, Iberia and Carthage in.
- `mode_hide_imperivm2_costs.vs` (Varios, Liga, Punic, Custom...) names 22 of the game's classes but its 51 orders do not exist in the game: no effect.
- The Liga gates (`rtemple_liga_gate_*`) are attached only to the Roman clones' classes.
- Starting resources (`bldForce.race == Germany`, 8 modes) is the same rule as the game's.

Now each rule is written for each of the mod's civilizations (`tools/lib/mode-rules.js`): the mode scripts (`mode_imperivm2_costs/gaul/iberia/rome.vs`, `mode_hide_imperivm2_costs.vs`, `mode_varios_disable_upgrades.vs`) are written once more per civilization as `<name>_<civ id>.vs`, with the clone's class ids (`GBarracks` -> `XBarracks`) and the order names the clone tool wrote in the civilization's `alias.commands` (`Barrack Level 2 Imp2 Cost` -> `Barrack Level 2 Imp2 Cost Gaul42`), and the modes start the copy of the player's civilization (`Mod_PlayerCiv(player)`); the game's civilizations get none.

- **`1 Varios`** hides the Liga upgrades for all eleven civilizations. **`5 Old school`** lets in Gaul 4.2, Iberia 4.2, Imperial Rome 4.2 and Numidian Carthage (the others, the game's included, are eliminated, as the mode did with the rest) and starts, for each, the Imperivm 2 costs and the kit of its civilization (Rome's, Gaul's, Iberia's). The branch the mode forced on Rome and Carthage (`mode_inverno_rules.vs`) is what Imperial Rome and Numidian Carthage are: it is not started.
- Those four keep the `Imp2 Cost` copies of their orders (`keepImp2` in `tools/greek-4-2.js`, an optional flag of `tools/lib/clone-civs.js`, the only change there: without it the clone tool drops them). Outside the mode the `groupverifier` scripts (the `_g42` copies of `verify_research`, `verify_cmdcost_building`, `verify_trainex`...) hide them, and the ones marked `NReqModeImp2` (only outside the mode) hide inside it.
- Names that do not exist for a clone (an order its branch hides, as Imperial Rome's `Roman Archers Imp2 Cost`, or one the mod's own Carthage lacks, `Forge javelins Imp2 Cost`) are asked of buildings that do not have them, as the mod's original script did for civilizations without them.
- **`modes/*.json`** (an SDK feature, `victory-modes.md`; the package ships them too): `1 VARIOS` (10,000 gold, population 150, no fog, explored map, bonus 2), `2 LIGA` (5,000, 100, fog, not explored, bonus 1), `3 PUNIC` (10,000, 100, no fog, explored, bonus 3) fix the room's settings for the mod's own modes, and `5 Old school` lets only `gaul42`, `iberia42`, `romeimp42` and `carthnum42` be chosen (the package listed `RomeImperial`, `Gaul`, `Iberia`, `CarthageNumidian`; here the four that stand for them). The room moves a player with another civilization to the first of the list; `5 Old school.VS` still eliminates one that gets in some other way. They need a DLL that has the feature (`dev-villoh`); without it they are ignored and the scripts do what they did.
- **Tried in game**: not (the maintainer's test of the modes was before this).

## The package's three rules, scoped to this mod's classes (tools/greek-4-2.js)

Gabbo's package turns on three rules: an army stays with its hero at the walls, wall sentries cannot be picked, civilians take no damage and cannot be attacked. Resurrected's `rules/game.json` has the three, but they are global (the engine patches its code once for every player, or tests a class name against every unit), so shipping the package's file would change the game's civilizations and any other mod's. The rule here is that what a civilization mod does reaches its own civilizations only, so:

- **Civilians that cannot be hurt**: `rules/game.json` lists only this port's own villager, old man and ambient classes (57: `XVillager`, `4WVillagerAmbient`, `HVillager`...), not `Peasant` or `PeasantAmbient`. The engine tests `IsKindOfName` (the class and what descends from it, read in the game's code, `0059c020`), so only these classes and their children are protected: the game's peasants, another mod's, and the neutral ones on the maps are not. The package also protected `Trader`, `Tradecar_Donkey`, `Camel` and `CartTrader`: those are the game's own classes (the clones have none of their own), so they stay as they are.
- **Sentries that cannot be picked**: `non_selectable="1"` on this port's own 45 Sentry classes (the same property `Wall`, `Tower` and `FakeTower` have). The engine's own rule sets a flag on every sentry when it is created; the game's pick filter (`PickFilter_IsSelectable`, `005e1b80`) refuses any object whose class has that property, whatever the object is, so it is class by class. What the engine rule also does and this does not: the flag keeps sentries out of the AI's armies (the code that reads it was not found in the export of `gbr.exe`: not confirmed).
- **An army that stays with its hero**: **not set**. It is one patch of the engine's code (the call to `DetachHero` in `CheckTargetTransportChange`, `00417dc0`): there is no class or civilization to look at. To do it without touching anybody else, the engine's rule needs a scope (for example `"armyStaysWithHero": { "classes": [...] }` tested with `IsKindOfName` on the unit, as `protectedClasses` is), a change in `imperivm-resurrected`, not here: proposed in imperivm-resurrected#50 (not implemented, and the detour at that site is untested). Until then the game's behavior (a unit that goes through a wall leaves its hero) is the mod's too.
- **Tried in game** (2026-10-26, profile with only this mod): the maintainer played the build and reported that everything is okay ("parece que todo está okey"). Not itemised: whether the 57 classes take no damage, whether the sentries cannot be clicked (and what is selected instead), and that nothing of the game's or of another mod is affected were not looked at one by one. The lists only name classes of this port; `validate-mod.js` accepts the file.
